About RoadWatch AI

RoadWatch AI is an academic deep learning demonstration designed to inspect digital road imagery, automatically detect potholes, and present bounding box annotations and confidence ratings.

Dataset Specification

  • Source: Road Damage Dataset 2022 (RDD2022), Seki Laboratory.
  • Target Defect: Class D40 - Pothole (mapped to class 0: pothole).
  • Partitioning: Reproducible 70% Train, 15% Validation, 15% Test split (seed: 42) with zero data leakage.
  • Negative Samples: Clean roadway images and non-pothole defect classes (D00, D10, D20, D30) preserved as background examples.

Neural Architecture

  • Model: Ultralytics YOLOv8n (Nano variant, ~3.1M parameters).
  • Resolution: 640 x 640 letterbox normalized inputs.
  • Loss Functions: CIoU Bounding Box Loss + Distribution Focal Loss (DFL) + BCE Objectness.
Academic Demonstration Disclaimer RoadWatch AI is developed strictly for educational research and technical prototyping purposes. The system does not guarantee roadway safety, structural road certification, or autonomous vehicular guidance.
